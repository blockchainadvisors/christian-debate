The Grand Procedure

From proposition to verdict, every debate follows a refined process that ensures fairness and depth.

I

Propose a Topic

Present your theological question or proposition to the forum. Frame it with clarity and precision, setting the stage for structured discourse. Every great debate begins with a well-formed question.

II

Declare Your Stance

Take a position and state it publicly. Whether you argue for, against, or remain undecided, your stance is recorded and tracked throughout the debate, honoring intellectual transparency.

III

Engage in Discourse

Build arguments with Scripture, reason, and tradition. Respond to counterpoints with substance. The multi-view system lets you engage through threads, side-by-side comparison, or curated exchanges.

IV

Vote with Reason

Cast reasoned votes across categories: logical rigor, scriptural support, rhetorical clarity, and charitable interpretation. Every vote demands a rationale, ensuring the best arguments rise to prominence.

V

Receive the Verdict

A neutral, community-driven verdict synthesizes all arguments and votes into a balanced assessment. Analytics reveal shifts in stance, the strength of key exchanges, and the overall arc of discourse.
