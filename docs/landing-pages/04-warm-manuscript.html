The Way of Discourse

A deliberate process that honors both conviction and humility

I

Declare Your Stance

Before entering the arena of ideas, declare where you stand. Your initial position is recorded, creating an honest foundation for the exchange that follows.

II

Present Your Case

Craft your argument with the rich text editor. Support your reasoning with scripture, logic, and evidence. Quality of thought is valued above volume of words.

III

Engage and Respond

Enter dialogue with those who see differently. Reply to specific points, acknowledge strong arguments, and refine your own position through the exchange.

IV

Vote with Reason

When you encounter a compelling argument, vote and explain why it moved you. Was it the scriptural basis? The logical clarity? A fresh perspective you had not considered?

V

Witness the Verdict

As the community engages, a neutral verdict emerges. Track how stances shift, which arguments proved most persuasive, and where genuine consensus forms.
