Section II

The Apparatus of Discourse

Four integrated views provide a comprehensive framework for examining every dimension of theological argumentation.

Thread View

Follow the full lineage of argumentation in its natural order. Nested replies preserve the dialectical structure, allowing readers to trace each line of reasoning from thesis to final rebuttal.

1 Threaded discourse mirrors the scholastic disputatio tradition.

Sides View

Arguments are systematically organized into pro and con columns, presenting the strongest case for each position. This bifurcated layout enables clear comparative analysis of competing claims.

2 Inspired by the Sic et Non method of Peter Abelard.

Neutral Verdict

Community-generated assessments synthesize the strongest arguments from both sides into an impartial summary. Reasoned voting with categorical evaluation ensures intellectual honesty.

3 Verdicts require a minimum quorum of qualified voters.

Analytics Engine

Track stance shifts, voting patterns, and argument effectiveness with quantitative precision. The analytics engine reveals how positions evolve through the weight of evidence and reason.

4 All metrics are anonymized and aggregated for privacy.

Section III

The Method of Inquiry

A structured methodology that transforms informal disagreement into productive theological scholarship.

I

Propose a Thesis

Frame your theological question or proposition with precision. A well-formed thesis includes scriptural grounding, historical context, and a clear position that invites reasoned dissent.

Cf. the quaestio disputata of the medieval university
II

Declare Your Stance

Participants declare their initial position before engaging. This transparency enables the platform to track genuine shifts in conviction—a rare and valuable metric in theological discourse.

Stance declarations are timestamped and immutable
III

Engage with Rigor

Present arguments across Thread and Sides views. The rich text editor supports scriptural citations, footnotes, and structured rebuttals. Best Exchanges surface the most substantive interactions.

Arguments are evaluated by logical coherence, not mere popularity
IV

Arrive at Verdict

Reasoned voting with categorical evaluation produces a Neutral Verdict. Voters must justify their assessment, and the analytics engine synthesizes patterns across the entire discourse.

Verdicts represent communal discernment, not mere majority rule
