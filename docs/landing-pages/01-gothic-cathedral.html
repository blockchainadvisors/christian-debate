The Order of Discourse

How It Works

Structured debate that rewards substance over volume.

I

Declare Your Stance

Enter each debate as Pro, Con, or Neutral. Your position is visible, fostering honesty and accountability in every argument you make.

II

Present Your Case

Compose arguments with a rich text editor. Cite scripture, reference scholarship, and build your reasoning with care and precision.

III

Vote with Reason

Every vote requires a category. Mark arguments as Persuasive, Factual, or a Strong Counter-Point — or flag them as Off-Topic or Ad Hominem. No silent drive-by voting.

IV

Watch Understanding Emerge

The platform surfaces the best exchanges, tracks stance shifts, and reveals where genuine persuasion occurs. Truth rises; noise recedes.
