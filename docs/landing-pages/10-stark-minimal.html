02 / Process

From question
to clarity

Three steps transform a divisive topic into a structured, productive conversation with measurable outcomes.

01
Declare Your Stance
Select a topic and publicly declare your starting position. This anchors the conversation and enables shift tracking as the debate unfolds.
Key Feature
Pre-debate stance survey with nuanced position options beyond binary agree/disagree
02
Engage With Reason
Present arguments with sources, vote on contributions using twelve distinct quality categories, and engage with opposing views directly.
Key Feature
Reasoned voting requires selecting a category, eliminating drive-by upvotes
03
Review the Verdict
The analytics engine compiles stance shifts, vote distributions, and argument quality into a transparent, community-driven neutral verdict.
Key Feature
Full analytics dashboard showing how minds changed and why
