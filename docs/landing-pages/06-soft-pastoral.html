How It Works

Four steps to deeper understanding

From question to clarity, every debate follows a gentle path designed to bring out the best thinking in your community.

1

Pose a Question

Start with something on your heart. Frame a theological question, an ethical dilemma, or a matter of interpretation. A good question is the seed of every meaningful conversation.

2

Declare Your Stance

Share where you stand and why. There is no pressure to have it all figured out -- honesty about uncertainty is just as welcome as firm conviction.

3

Engage with Grace

Respond to others with thoughtfulness. Use reasoned voting to affirm strong arguments. The multi-view system helps you see the full picture as the debate unfolds.

4

Discover the Verdict

Once the conversation matures, the Neutral Verdict synthesizes all sides into a balanced summary. Analytics reveal patterns of agreement and where the community truly stands.
