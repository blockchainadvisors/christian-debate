02

How It Works

Process flow // step-by-step execution

01

Declare Your Stance

Enter a debate and plant your flag. Pro, Con, or Undecided. Your position is public. This is about intellectual honesty, not anonymous sniping.

02

Build Your Argument

Rich text editor. Scripture references. Logical structuring. Craft arguments that deserve engagement, not hot takes that deserve deletion.

03

Engage & Vote With Reason

Rebut. Counter. Concede points. Vote on arguments by category. The system surfaces the strongest exchanges automatically.

04

Receive the Verdict

A neutral, data-driven verdict synthesizes the debate. Strongest arguments highlighted. Stance shifts recorded. Truth pursued, not just won.
