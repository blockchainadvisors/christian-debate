// System Modules

Debate Architecture

Six interlocking systems designed to transform how Christians engage with theology, doctrine, and the questions that define faith.

Thread View

Classic threaded conversations with deep nesting, real-time updates, and virtualized rendering for debates that scale to thousands of replies.

Sides View

Pro and Con columns displayed side by side. See the strongest arguments for each position emerge organically through community reasoning.

Best Exchanges

The most impactful argument-counterargument pairs, surfaced by collective intelligence. The highlights reel of every debate.

Neutral Verdict

AI-generated impartial summaries that synthesize both sides, identify common ground, and present the state of the discourse without bias.

Reasoned Voting

Every vote requires a reason and category -- "well-sourced," "logically sound," "novel insight." Votes carry weight beyond simple up/down.

Analytics Engine

Stance shift tracking, argument quality metrics, engagement heatmaps, and persuasion analytics. Data-driven discourse at every level.

// Execution Protocol

How It Works

From initialization to verdict -- a four-phase process engineered for depth.

01
> init --topic
// declare your stance
> stance.set("pro")
OK Registered.

Declare Position

Enter a debate and declare your stance. Your position is logged for shift tracking.

»
02
> compose --mode rich
// scripture + logic
> cite("Rom 8:28")
OK Linked.

Build Arguments

Craft your case with rich text, scripture references, and structured reasoning.

»
03
> vote --reasoned
// category: "well-sourced"
> submit(weight: 1.3)
OK Counted.

Evaluate & Vote

Review arguments across all views. Cast reasoned votes with specific categories.

»
04
> verdict --generate
// neutral synthesis
> analytics.render()
COMPLETE

Verdict & Insights

Receive a neutral AI verdict, view stance shifts, and explore full analytics.
