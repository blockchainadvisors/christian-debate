Pillars of the Platform

Four foundational views that transform debate from noise into illumination.

🗣

Thread View

Follow the full conversation as it unfolds, with nested replies, inline citations, and real-time stance indicators beside each contribution.

⚖

Sides View

See Pro and Con arguments laid out side by side. Quickly grasp the strongest case for each position without scrolling through tangents.

★

Best Exchanges

Community-surfaced moments where two debaters genuinely engaged each other's points, ranked by quality of reasoning and charitable interpretation.

⚗

Neutral Verdict

Impartial summaries that weigh evidence from both sides, highlighting where consensus was reached and where honest disagreement remains.

✎

Reasoned Voting

Every vote requires a category: "Strong Evidence," "Sound Logic," "Charitable Tone," or "Novel Insight." No drive-by upvotes allowed.

↺

Stance Tracking

Declare your position before engaging, then update it as the debate progresses. The platform tracks genuine shifts in understanding over time.

📊

Analytics Engine

Debate health scores, participation balance, argument quality metrics, and trend analysis reveal patterns invisible to the naked eye.

🌐

Federation

Hub-and-spoke architecture lets churches and communities run their own sites while connecting into a broader network of Christian discourse.

The Way of Dialogue

From proposition to verdict, every debate follows a path designed for clarity and grace.

I

A Question is Posed

A member frames a proposition clearly and charitably. They set the scope, provide context from Scripture or tradition, and invite both sides to respond.

Proposition Phase
II

Stances are Declared

Participants declare their initial position before engaging. This honest starting point becomes part of the record, creating accountability and transparency.

Declaration Phase
III

Structured Exchange

Arguments flow through Thread View while the platform organizes them into Sides View automatically. Reasoned votes surface the strongest contributions in real time.

Dialogue Phase
IV

Verdict and Reflection

When debate matures, a neutral verdict crystallizes the outcome. Analytics reveal who shifted their stance, which arguments resonated, and what wisdom emerged.

Resolution Phase
