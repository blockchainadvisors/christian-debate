PastorMike Oak Side A 2 days ago
The principle of Sola Scriptura holds that Scripture alone is the final authority for faith and practice. As 2 Timothy 3:16-17 states, "All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness, so that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work." The key phrase here is thoroughly equipped -- suggesting sufficiency.
Hermeneutics Authority
SC
SisterCatherine Elder Side B 2 days ago
"Thoroughly equipped" does not necessarily mean "exclusively equipped."
Consider that Paul also wrote in 2 Thessalonians 2:15, "Hold to the traditions which you were taught, whether by word of mouth or by letter." This explicitly references oral tradition alongside written Scripture. The early Church operated for decades before the New Testament canon was established.

PastorMike Oak Side A 1 day ago
Fair point about the timeline. However, the traditions Paul refers to were apostolic teachings later inscripturated. Once the canon was closed, the written Word became the normative standard. Tradition is valuable for interpretation but is not co-equal with Scripture as an authority.
